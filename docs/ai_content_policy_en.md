# OmniChat AI-Generated Content Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-16_

## Disclaimer

OmniChat is an interface to third-party large language models (LLMs). **All responses you receive are generated by AI and may be inaccurate, outdated, biased, or inappropriate.** Always verify important information before relying on it. AI-generated content does not reflect the views of the app developers or the model providers.

## How the app helps you review AI content

- **AI Team / reasoning sections** — the app shows reasoning and multi-agent ("AI Team") collaboration steps in collapsible sections so you can inspect how a response was produced.
- **Export watermark** — content exported from the app may carry an "AI Generated" watermark.
- **Search citations** — when the assistant uses web search, sources are displayed so you can verify claims.

## Reporting harmful or inappropriate content

OmniChat connects to third-party model providers; the app itself does not generate or host content, and it cannot remove content from those providers' systems. If you encounter content that you believe is harmful, illegal, or otherwise violates the applicable model provider's policies, you can:

1. **Stop using the conversation** — delete the conversation from your local history at any time.
2. **Report to the model provider** — use the reporting channel provided by the provider that generated the content (for example OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, DeepSeek, or OpenRouter).
3. **Report the app** — if the issue relates to the app itself, open an issue at:
   https://github.com/JasonMMIV/OmniChat/issues

## Content moderation

OmniChat is a client-side application: content is generated by the third-party provider you selected, and moderation is governed by that provider's policies and systems. The app does not filter or moderate model output beyond what the provider itself applies.
